Control video playback speed like a pro with keyboard shortcuts and intuitive controls.

Default Keyboard Shortcuts

  • + or = Increase playback speed
  • - or _ Decrease playback speed
  • * (Numpad) Reset to 1x speed
  • Shift + Mouse Wheel Adjust with scroll
