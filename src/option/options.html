Welcome!

Thank you for installing YouTube Playback Speed Control! This extension allows you to control video playback speed with convenient keyboard shortcuts.

Default Keyboard Shortcuts

  • + or = - Increase playback speed
  • - or _ - Decrease playback speed
  • * (Numpad) - Reset to 1x speed
  • Shift + Mouse Wheel - Adjust speed with mouse

On-Screen Controls

A small control panel will appear on your videos with:

  • >> button to increase speed
  • Current speed display (click to reset to 1x)
  • << button to decrease speed
  • ⚙️ button to open settings

Getting Started

  1. Visit any YouTube video
  2. Use keyboard shortcuts or on-screen controls to adjust speed
  3. Customize your experience in the Settings tab
  4. Enable "All Videos" mode to use on other HTML5 video players
Tip: You can customize all keyboard shortcuts, speed increments, and display options in the Settings tab!

Features

  • Customizable keyboard shortcuts for speed control
  • Mouse wheel support with Shift modifier
  • Works on YouTube and other HTML5 video players
  • Multiple display modes and positions
  • Remembers your preferred speed across videos
  • Tracks time saved from watching at higher speeds
  • Fully compatible with embedded videos

Compatibility

  • YouTube (regular and embedded)
  • HTML5 video players (when enabled)
  • Chrome 88+ and Edge 88+
